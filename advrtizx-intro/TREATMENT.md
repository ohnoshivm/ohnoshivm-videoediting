# AdvrtizX — "X MARKS THE MARKET"
60-second intro film · 1920×1080 · 30 fps · 1800 frames · red #E80101 / white / near-black #0A0A0A

## The idea
An X is the only mark that is defined by its single point: two lines, one intersection.
In every real-estate market on earth, there is one point where a buyer's attention and a
property meet. AdvrtizX is the intelligence that finds that point — in any city, any
language, at any scale. The X in the name *is* the method.

Visual logic: the whole film is drawn with **two lines that cross**. Cities are drawn as line
grids (streets). Buyer attention is drawn as red sightlines. Where they converge, an X locks.
X's stack into diagrid towers (real architecture made of X's). Every X in the world collapses
into one X, and that X becomes the AdvrtizX mark. Open and close are the same gesture: two
lines crossing. Swap another agency's logo in and the film stops making sense.

Rules: only lines, type and the mark. No particles, no dashboards, no stock imagery, no glow
haze. Precision over decoration: hairlines, exact geometry, coordinates, typographic scale.
