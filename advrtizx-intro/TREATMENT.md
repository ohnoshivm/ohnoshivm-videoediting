# DOMINANT: treatment

60-second intro for **AdvrtizX** (AI-first advertising agency for real estate, worldwide). 1920x1080, 30 fps, 1800 frames (f0-f1799), 112.5 BPM: beat = 16 f, bar = 64 f, 16th = 4 f, so every musical subdivision lands on a frame. Every frame cue is in `cue_sheet.json` (single source of truth for sync with the score).

## The idea
Skylines are sold before they are built: every tower on Earth rises because someone sold it first, with an ad. AdvrtizX is the one intelligence that sells them, in any city, any language, all at once. The film builds the world's skylines at impossible speed; every tower is crowned with one of the two shapes of the AdvrtizX monogram, the A-wedge or the D. At the climax the world's skyline lights up as the mark itself, and the mark becomes the last word of the closing sentence: **EVERY SKYLINE STARTS WITH AN [AD]**. The logo is the word "ad".
Sonic idea: A is the dominant of D. From the first impact the score sits on an A pedal and refuses to resolve for 46 s. The logo lands as a perfect cadence, A then D.

## Logo (source of truth: `assets/mark.svg`, plus `3.png` white on red, `4.png` red on white)
Two paths `A` and `D`, fill #E80101, viewBox `7.5 0 219.7 100` (2.197:1). A: counterless "half-A" right trapezoid, left edge rises 3:4 (53.13 deg), flat top, vertical right edge, fillets R7.5 (bottom-left) and R10.8 (top-left), one shallow arch bite in the base (circle r 21.2, chord 32.0, 7.3 tall). D: 44.1 rectangle plus a true semicircle r 50 (counter filled). A 7.6-unit slot separates them. Brand red #E80101. There is no X; the name is written "AdvrtizX".

## Look: RED ATMOSPHERE
Monochrome world. Sky and fog are brand red (232,1,1). Architecture is white, lit by a hard white key with red ambient bounce; distance dissolves into red. White type on red; SOLD signs are red letters on white boards. Only red, white and the tones lighting and fog mix between them: no black, grey or other hues; no glow/neon, flares, bokeh, decorative particles, HUDs or AI imagery. Real 3D with perspective lenses (wide for scale, long for compression). Cinematic camera: cranes, low-angle tilts, lateral trucks, whip-pans, orbits, one extreme pull-back. Motion blur on everything that moves, deterministic camera shake on impacts, subtle film grain.

## Architecture grammar
Every tower is a white rectangular shaft with subtle floor lines topped by a crown that is one of the monogram's shapes extruded with its exact profile: the A-wedge (fillets and bite included, may be mirrored) or the D (a dome with the arc up, or in logo orientation as a half-dome to the side). Towers rise and land with impacts. Nothing ever falls, breaks or collapses (sensitivity rule: no collapsing towers, no aircraft, no debris clouds).

## Type
One premium heavy grotesk for Latin (Mona Sans Variable, uppercase, 800-900, tight tracking, massive), weight-matched Noto Black faces for other scripts. Type lives in 3D (standing in the fog, partly occluded by towers) or slams full-frame in 2D. Hero type never limply fades in: it slams, rises out of a mask, or is revealed by architecture.

## Avoid
Any X motif; maps, street grids, globes, plan views; drafting annotations (dimension strings, north arrows, scale bars, coordinates, monospace labels, hairline line drawing); split-flap text; sun/clock/time-of-day motifs; staircases; doors or 9:16 phone frames; feeds of cliche ads; 53.13-degree wipes; "red flooding over white" reveals. Fonts: Archivo, IBM Plex Mono, Instrument Serif, Playfair, Montserrat, Poppins, Inter, Roboto, Bebas, Oswald.
